## What the library contains

The media is a **documentary construction and interiors library**, not a stock
campaign. It includes:

- finished ceilings, feature walls, staircases and interior fit-outs
- site progress: foundations, columns, rebar, formwork, concrete pours and roads
- residential/commercial exteriors and real-estate context
- a small number of team/site photographs
- material samples, a board flyer, a services flyer and a social-media collage
- packaging and installation videos, a cathedral ceiling film and a talking-head clip

This supports a warm, editorial, evidence-led direction. It does **not** support
a glossy luxury-property aesthetic or a pure product catalogue.
